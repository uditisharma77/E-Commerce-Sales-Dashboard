# E-Commerce Sales Dashboard Project

Recently built an **E-Commerce Sales Dashboard Project** using Excel tools like **Power Query, Power Pivot, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers** to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

The dashboard not only visualizes sales performance but also helps identify customer behaviour patterns, operational efficiency, seasonal demand trends, and market opportunities for strategic business decisions.

## 🔧 What I Used in This Project

- Data Extraction & Cleaning
- Data Transformation using **Power Query Editor**
- Data Modelling and DAX formulas using **Power Pivot**
- Analysis using **Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts**
- Interactive filtering using **Slicers**
- Dashboard creation & KPI visualization in Excel

## 📊 Key Insights from the Dashboard

### 💰 Total Revenue Generated: ₹41.99 Lakhs

The business achieved strong overall sales performance.

### 🚚 Average Delivery Time: 3.18 Days

Indicates an efficient delivery system and good operational performance.

### 🛒 Top Revenue-Generating Category: Electronics

Electronics emerged as the top revenue-generating category, showing strong customer demand for tech products.

### 📍 Top Performing City: Delhi

Delhi was the strongest market in terms of revenue performance.

### 📅 Highest Sales Months: February, August, and November

Sales spikes suggest strong seasonal demand and/or promotional impact during these months.

### 🎯 Customer Buying Factors

- **Ad Influence (28%)** had the highest impact on purchases
- **Quality (20%)**
- **Brand Trust (17%)**

This shows that **marketing and product perception strongly affect customer decisions**.

### 💳 Payment Preference

**Online Payments (52%)** slightly exceeded **Cash on Delivery (48%)**, reflecting growing digital payment adoption.

### 📆 Order Trends by Weekday

The highest number of orders were recorded on **Sunday and Tuesday**, indicating peak customer activity during these days.

### 👥 Customer Behaviour

Although male customers placed a higher number of orders, female customers generated greater overall revenue, indicating a higher average spending pattern among female buyers.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

### 1. Cross-Sell to Electronics Customers

**Electronics dominates product sales.** Increase cross-selling and upselling opportunities among Electronics customers.

### 2. Prepare for Seasonal Demand

Revenue spikes in **February, August, and November** suggest seasonality and/or promotional influence. Plan **inventory, advertising, and logistics** in advance during high-demand periods.

### 3. Encourage Prepaid Orders

**COD usage remains high at 48%**, even though online payments slightly lead.

Offer **cashback or discounts on prepaid orders** to encourage digital payments and potentially reduce return risks and operational costs associated with COD.

### 4. Leverage Weekend Demand

**Sunday records one of the highest order volumes.** Schedule flash sales and targeted weekend promotions to maximize customer engagement.

### 5. Expand into Mid-Performing Cities

Increase marketing efforts in mid-performing cities to diversify revenue sources and reduce dependence on the strongest markets.

### 6. Invest in Targeted Marketing

**Ad Influence accounts for the largest share (28%) among purchase reasons.** Invest in targeted digital marketing campaigns and influencer strategies to improve customer acquisition and conversion.
